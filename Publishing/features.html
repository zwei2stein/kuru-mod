Kuru

Neural degradation caused by eating meat contaminated with misfolded prions. Known as the laughing sickness.

Causes mood instability with chances of various mental breaks. Debilitates pawns, making tasks more difficult. Progressively damages the brain, causing pain and loss of consciousness. Eventually fatal.
  • Transmission: Eating human meat (cooking does NOT prevent)
  • Mental breaks: Laughing Fit, Wander Confused, Insane Ramblings, Insulting Spree
  • Capacity reductions: Talking -10%, Moving -10%, Manipulation -10%
  • Brain damage: Accumulates over time, eventually fatal

Brain Damage

Permanent brain injury caused by Kuru disease progression. Represents the cumulative effects of prion damage to neural tissue.

Cannot be healed naturally. Requires advanced treatment methods.
  • Permanent injury
  • Can be fatal if brain is destroyed
  • Can be healed: By scar removal methods or complete brain destruction + revival

Natural Cannibal

An archite gene carried by pawns who have adapted to prion diseases and eating raw human meat.

Provides resistance to Kuru disease - carriers will eventually have the disease removed by their immune system.
  • Kuru resistance: Immune system detects and removes prion diseases
  • Food poisoning resistance: 10% reduction (0.9x factor)
  • Immunity gain speed: +10%
  • Conditional bonuses (if infected):
  • +10% Move Speed
  • +10% Work Speed Global
  • +10% Social Impact
